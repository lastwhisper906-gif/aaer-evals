- **not excluded:** R2 verified Beneish *coefficients* and published AUC/ρ/p (all sourced from baseline_table — unaffected); PKT-R2 grep has no m_flag/unified item; no D-P/ERRATA entry mentions it.
- **fix shape:** flip `synthesis.py` to the frozen rule (`m > M_FLAG`), regenerate; but `unified_table.csv` is a committed published artifact → correction goes through the disclose-don't-revise channel (ERRATA entry + regeneration; whether E-00x or a lighter note is owner's call).
