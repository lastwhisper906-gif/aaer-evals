## FINDINGS (BACKLOG format R6-nn, ranked by claim impact)

### R6-01 "Structurally impossible" / "impossible by construction" overstates the holdout premise — it rests on the vendor-declared cutoff and on a name-pinned endpoint being a static checkpoint, neither verifiable; the published fuzzy-boundary caveat wrongly localizes this to HUBG only
- category: DOCS (premise wording) · size: S · status: NEW
- **steelman first**: the design already contains the right instrument — the recognition gate at k=5 with a pre-committed ≥2/5 revocation rule and two positive controls that fired correctly (ISSUE_2 §2). That is genuinely strong *empirical* evidence of non-recognition. And ISSUE_2 §4 honestly flags HUBG's +5-day boundary.
- **the attack that lands**: `README.md:59` ("memorization is structurally impossible"), `METHOD.md:66-68` ("the structurally impossible axis"), `ISSUE_2:18-19` ("*structurally impossible* to have been memorized because it did not exist when the model was trained") and `:68-69` ("impossible **by construction**") assert a logical guarantee. The guarantee's premises: (i) the declared 2026-01 knowledge cutoff is accurate, and (ii) the endpoint answering to the pinned *name* `claude-sonnet-5` in July 2026 (scoring runs 2026-07-07/09) served a checkpoint whose training data still ended 2026-01. INV-21 pins the model **name** and CLI version — not weights. A served-snapshot refresh or continued post-training between 2026-03 and 2026-07 would put *all three* revelations (2026-02-05/02-20/03-12) inside reachable training data — yet the published caveat (`ISSUE_2:149-151`) covers HUBG alone, implying WMK/GNE are safe *by construction*. They are safe *by measurement* (the gate), which is a different and honest claim.
- **failure scenario**: a vendor model-refresh announcement (or an expert reviewer who knows name-pinned endpoints aren't checkpoint-pinned) demonstrates the "impossible" wording was never justified; the flagship "clean test" framing takes the credibility hit — even though the k=5 gate evidence would have survived intact under honest wording.
- **fix shape**: XS wording repair on the two living surfaces (README:59, METHOD §Memorization): "where the revelation postdates the evaluatee's *declared* training cutoff **and** measured non-recognition was demonstrated (k=5 gate, positive controls)". ISSUE_2 is frozen — generalize its HUBG caveat via the existing post-publication-notice channel (owner-gated packet, D99 precedent). No re-run, no machinery.
- claim: holdout rows 5–7; README/METHOD holdout framing.

### R6-02 The published FPR bias-direction claim is one-sided — hindsight-certified control selection (E5's cutoff+3y window, E4's 2026 AAER index) pushes specificity the *other* way; net direction is unknown, not "downward"
- category: STATS-DISCLOSURE · size: S · status: NEW (PKT class — RESULTS rows are signature-gated)
- **evidence**: `RESULTS.md:23` (row 8): "the bias direction pushes specificity downward" — unconditional; `ISSUE_0` §7.3 same direction ("conservative"). But `CONTROL_CRITERIA_v1.md` §3 E5 excludes any control with an 8-K Item 4.02 in **[cutoff − 5y, cutoff + 3y]** — up to three years of *post-cutoff* outcome knowledge — and E4 screens against the full AAER index as collected in 2026. The undetected-fraud effect (Dyck et al., ISSUE_0 §7.3) pushes the opposite way. Both are real; the net sign is unknown.
