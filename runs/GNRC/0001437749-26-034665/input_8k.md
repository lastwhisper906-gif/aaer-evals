Generac Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results

[0001437749-26-033685:8k_2_02:5]
Further momentum in the C&I segment drives significant backlog increase; Residential segment returns to strong growth in the quarter supported by lower power quality mega-trends

[0001437749-26-033685:8k_2_02:6]
WAUKESHA, WISCONSIN (October 28, 2026) – Generac Holdings Inc. (NYSE: GNRC) (“Generac” or the “Company”), a leading global designer and manufacturer of energy technology solutions and other power products, today reported financial results for its third quarter ended September 30, 2026, and provided an update on its outlook for the full year 2026.

[0001437749-26-033685:8k_2_02:7]
Third Quarter 2026 Highlights

[0001437749-26-033685:8k_2_02:8]
| ● | Net sales increased 16% to a record $1.29 billion during the third quarter of 2026 as compared to $1.11 billion in the prior year third quarter. Acquisitions, divestitures and foreign currency had a net favorable impact of 1% to sales growth during the quarter. |

[0001437749-26-033685:8k_2_02:9]
|  | - | Commercial & Industrial (“C&I”) segment external net sales increased approximately 28% to $552 million as compared to $432 million in the prior year. |

[0001437749-26-033685:8k_2_02:10]
|  | - | Residential segment external net sales increased approximately 9% to $743 million as compared to $682 million in the prior year. |

[0001437749-26-033685:8k_2_02:11]
| ● | Net income attributable to the Company during the third quarter was $109 million, or $1.83 per share, as compared to $66 million, or $1.12 per share, for the same period of 2025. |

[0001437749-26-033685:8k_2_02:12]
| ● | Adjusted net income attributable to the Company, as defined in the accompanying reconciliation schedules, was $151 million, or $2.54 per share, as compared to $108 million, or $1.83 per share, in the third quarter of 2025. |

[0001437749-26-033685:8k_2_02:13]
| ● | Adjusted EBITDA before deducting for noncontrolling interests, as defined in the accompanying reconciliation schedules, was $256 million, or 19.8% of net sales, as compared to $193 million, or 17.3% of net sales, in the prior year. |

[0001437749-26-033685:8k_2_02:14]
| ● | Cash flow from operations was $171 million as compared to $118 million in the prior year. Free cash flow, as defined in the accompanying reconciliation schedules, was $132 million as compared to $96 million in the third quarter of 2025. |

[0001437749-26-033685:8k_2_02:15]
| ● | As previously disclosed during the quarter, the Company entered into an agreement with Amazon.com, Inc. that included the execution of a long-term supply agreement for backup power generators for Amazon data centers. Initial deliveries are expected to total a combined $2.4 billion in 2027 and 2028. |

[0001437749-26-033685:8k_2_02:16]
| ● | The Company is maintaining its full-year 2026 net sales growth guidance in the mid-to-high teens percent range as compared to the prior year. Full-year 2026 adjusted EBITDA margin, before deducting for non-controlling interests, is now expected to be approximately 20.5 to 21.0% as compared to the previous guidance range of 20.0 to 21.0%. |

[0001437749-26-033685:8k_2_02:17]
“I’m incredibly proud of our team’s efforts as our third quarter results reflect an all-time record for net sales as we continued to see tremendous momentum in our C&I segment during the third quarter with significant additional commitments from data center customers providing further confidence in our position as a leading provider to this large and rapidly growing market,” said Aaron Jagdfeld, President and Chief Executive Officer. “Following the recently announced agreement with Amazon, along with other wins from both new and existing customers, our backlog for products to be sold to the data center market has now grown to approximately $4.5 billion, and we continue to execute on our previously announced capacity expansion plans that will allow us to capture this generational growth opportunity.”

[0001437749-26-033685:8k_2_02:18]
Jagdfeld continued, “Additionally, our Residential segment returned to strong year-over-year growth during the quarter as the mega-trends around lower power quality supported an impressive increase in shipments of home standby and portable generators relative to the prior year period.”

[0001437749-26-033685:8k_2_02:19]
Additional Third Quarter 2026 Consolidated Highlights

[0001437749-26-033685:8k_2_02:20]
Gross profit margin was 39.9% as compared to 38.3% in the prior year third quarter. The increase was primarily driven by favorable price/cost impacts in the current year quarter, including lower tariff costs, partially offset by unfavorable sales mix.

[0001437749-26-033685:8k_2_02:21]
Operating expenses increased by $31.6 million, or 10%, as compared to the third quarter of 2025. The increase was primarily driven by increased operating expense investments to support future C&I growth, as well as higher marketing spend to drive awareness for our products and additional incentive compensation and intangible amortization expense, partially offset by lower operating expense investment in energy technology solutions.

[0001437749-26-033685:8k_2_02:22]
Provision for income taxes for the current year quarter was $34.2 million, or an effective tax rate of 23.9%, as compared to $11.8 million, or a 15.0% effective tax rate, for the prior year quarter. The increase in the effective tax rate was primarily driven by certain favorable discrete tax items in the prior year quarter that did not repeat in the current year.

[0001437749-26-033685:8k_2_02:23]
Cash flow from operations was $171.0 million during the third quarter, as compared to $118.4 million in the prior year. Free cash flow, as defined in the accompanying reconciliation schedules, was $131.7 million as compared to $96.5 million in the third quarter of 2025. This strong increase was primarily driven by higher operating earnings, partially offset by increased capital expenditures.

[0001437749-26-033685:8k_2_02:24]
Third Quarter Business Segment Results

[0001437749-26-033685:8k_2_02:25]
Commercial & Industrial Segment

[0001437749-26-033685:8k_2_02:26]
Commercial & Industrial segment total sales increased approximately 28% to $553.0 million from $432.4 million in the prior year quarter, including an approximate 4% net favorable impact to sales growth from the combination of acquisitions, divestitures, and foreign currency. The core total sales growth for the segment was primarily driven by ramping revenue from products sold into the global data center market. In addition, broad-based regional growth internationally was offset by lower shipments to the domestic industrial distributor and telecom channels.

[0001437749-26-033685:8k_2_02:27]
Adjusted EBITDA for the segment, before deducting for noncontrolling interests, was $69.3 million, or 12.5% of C&I total sales, as compared to $43.8 million, or 10.1% of total sales, in the prior year. This margin increase was primarily driven by operating leverage on higher shipments and the net favorable impact of acquisitions/divestitures.

[0001437749-26-033685:8k_2_02:28]
Residential Segment

[0001437749-26-033685:8k_2_02:29]
Residential segment total sales increased approximately 9% to $744.1 million as compared to $685.0 million in the prior year quarter. This increase was primarily driven by strong sales growth for home standby and portable generators compared to the prior year, partially offset by lower shipments of energy storage systems.

[0001437749-26-033685:8k_2_02:30]
Adjusted EBITDA for the segment was $200.8 million, or 27.0% of Residential segment total sales, as compared to $160.2 million, or 23.4% of Residential sales, in the prior year. This margin increase was primarily driven by favorable price/cost impacts, operating leverage given higher shipment volumes, and lower operating expense investment in energy technology solutions.

[0001437749-26-033685:8k_2_02:31]
2026 Outlook

[0001437749-26-033685:8k_2_02:32]
The Company is maintaining its full-year 2026 net sales growth guidance in the mid-to-high teens percent range as compared to the prior year, including an approximate 2% favorable impact from the net effect of foreign currency, acquisitions, and divestitures.

[0001437749-26-033685:8k_2_02:33]
Additionally, the Company is maintaining its guidance for net income margin, before deducting for non-controlling interests, of approximately 9.0 to 10.0% for the full-year 2026. The corresponding adjusted EBITDA margin guidance is now expected to be approximately 20.5 to 21.0% as compared to the previous guidance range of approximately 20.0 to 21.0%.

About Generac

[0001437749-26-033685:8k_2_02:39]
Generac is a total energy solutions company that empowers people to use energy on their own terms. Founded in 1959, Generac is a leading global designer, manufacturer, and provider of a wide range of energy technology solutions. The Company provides power generation equipment, energy storage systems, energy management devices & solutions, and other power products and services serving the residential, commercial, data center, telecom, rental, and industrial markets. The Company’s broad portfolio of energy technology offerings for homes and businesses enables its mission to Power a Smarter World and lead the evolution to more resilient, efficient, and innovative energy solutions.
