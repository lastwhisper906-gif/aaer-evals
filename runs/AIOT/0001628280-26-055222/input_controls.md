Item 4. Controls and Procedures

[0001628280-26-055222:item_4_controls:2]
a. Disclosure controls and procedures.

[0001628280-26-055222:item_4_controls:3]
Our management, with the participation of our Chief Executive Officer and Chief Financial Officer, evaluated the effectiveness of our disclosure controls and procedures (as defined in Rules 13a-15(e) and 15d-15(e) under the Exchange Act) as of June 30, 2026.

[0001628280-26-055222:item_4_controls:4]
Based on this evaluation, our Chief Executive Officer and Chief Financial Officer concluded that our disclosure controls and procedures were effective as of June 30, 2026. Our disclosure controls and procedures are designed to provide reasonable assurance that information required to be disclosed in the reports we file or submit under the Exchange Act is:

[0001628280-26-055222:item_4_controls:5]
•recorded, processed, summarized and reported within the time periods specified in the SEC rules and forms; and

[0001628280-26-055222:item_4_controls:6]
•accumulated and communicated to management, including the Chief Executive Officer and Chief Financial Officer, as appropriate, to allow timely decisions regarding required disclosure.

[0001628280-26-055222:item_4_controls:7]
b. Changes in internal control over financial reporting.

[0001628280-26-055222:item_4_controls:8]
There were no changes in our internal control over financial reporting during the quarter ended June 30, 2026 that have materially affected, or are reasonably likely to materially affect, our internal control over financial reporting.
