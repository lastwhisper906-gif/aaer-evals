DALLAS, October 28, 2026  – Lennox (NYSE: LII), a leader in energy-efficient building and home comfort solutions, today reported third-quarter revenue of $1.6 billion, up 10%. GAAP operating income was $312 million, with an operating margin of 19.8%. Total segment profit¹ was $323 million, down 2%, and total segment margin declined 240 basis points to 20.6%. GAAP diluted earnings per share were $6.41, and adjusted diluted earnings per share were $6.84, down 7%.

[0001069202-26-000094:8k_2_02:14]
“In the third-quarter, Building Climate Solutions continued to deliver strong revenue growth and residential end-markets began stabilizing. However, our margins and results fell short of our expectations, reflecting heightened competitive pressure and the impact of persistent inflation on our costs,” said Alok Maskara, Chief Executive Officer. “We are retooling our processes to more effectively pass through cost increases while accelerating productivity. Despite the near-term challenges, we remain confident in our strategy and the long-term resilience of our business model.”

[0001069202-26-000094:8k_2_02:15]
Home Comfort Solutions revenue increased 3% in the third quarter, while segment margin declined 490 basis points. Targeted price adjustments in select market segments were implemented to defend and recapture share. Those actions, combined with inflation and tariff-related costs weighed on profitability. Looking ahead, priorities include mitigating share challenges, advancing productivity and tariff mitigation initiatives, and introducing new products in the entry-tier portfolio.

[0001069202-26-000094:8k_2_02:16]
Building Climate Solutions revenue increased 24% in the third quarter, including organic growth of 15%, with continued strength across commercial HVAC, refrigeration, and services. Segment margin remained strong at 26.2%, despite a 110 basis point decline from record margins in the prior-year period. Growth was fueled by business development across the segment, and we still expect full year margins to expand despite inflation and tariff pressures.
