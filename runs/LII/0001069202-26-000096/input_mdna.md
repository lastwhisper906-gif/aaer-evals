[0001069202-26-000096:mdna:17]

[0001069202-26-000096:mdna:18]
Following this ruling, the U.S. presidential administration imposed a temporary surcharge under Section 122, which applied a 10% global tariff on most imported products, effective through July 24, 2026. The temporary surcharge was replaced by a new Section 301 forced labor tariffs framework of 10% or 12.5%, covering imports from 60 trading partners. Section 232 articles and qualifying USMCA goods are excluded from this new framework. Section 232 tariffs also continued to evolve, with modifications implemented in April and June 2026. The Company continues to evaluate tariff mitigation strategies.
