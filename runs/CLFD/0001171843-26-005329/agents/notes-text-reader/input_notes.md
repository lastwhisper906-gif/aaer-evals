In September 2025, the FASB issued ASU No. 2025-06, Targeted Improvements to the Accounting for Internal-Use Software. The ASU replaces the current stage-based capitalization model with a principles-based approach that permits capitalization of internal-use software costs once (1) management has authorized and committed to fund the project, and (2) it is probable the project will be completed and the software will function as intended. Costs incurred while significant development uncertainty exists must be expensed as incurred. ASU 2025-06 can be applied prospectively, retrospectively, or using a modified retrospective approach. The Company is evaluating the impact of this standard on our financial statements and related disclosures, including the Company’s adoption approach for ASU 2025-06 and anticipates adopting the standard for the annual period starting October 1, 2028.
