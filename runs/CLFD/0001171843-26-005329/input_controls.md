# CLFD 10-Q controls and the auditor's report


## item_4_controls

ITEM 4. CONTROLS AND PROCEDURES

[0001171843-26-005329:item_4_controls:2]
Evaluation of Disclosure Controls and Procedures

[0001171843-26-005329:item_4_controls:3]
The Company’s management carried out an evaluation, under the supervision and with the participation of the Company’s Chief Executive Officer and the Company’s Chief Financial Officer, of the effectiveness of the design and operation of the Company’s disclosure controls and procedures (as such term is defined in Rule 13a-15(e) under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended) as of June 30, 2026. Based upon that evaluation, the Company’s Chief Executive Officer and the Company’s Chief Financial Officer concluded that the Company’s disclosure controls and procedures were effective.

[0001171843-26-005329:item_4_controls:4]
Changes in Internal Control over Financial Reporting

[0001171843-26-005329:item_4_controls:5]
There were no changes in the Company’s internal control over financial reporting that occurred during the three months ended June 30, 2026 that have materially affected, or are reasonably likely to materially affect, the Company’s internal control over financial reporting.
