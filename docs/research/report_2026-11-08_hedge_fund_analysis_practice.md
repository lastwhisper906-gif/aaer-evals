# 포렌식 분석가는 필링을 뒤에서부터 읽고 현금으로 검증한다

헤지펀드와 포렌식 공매도 분석가가 미국 상장사의 10-Q/10-K를 다루는 방식은, 공개된 1차 자료를 모으면 한 문장으로 요약된다: **가장 덜 가공된 문서(필링 본문, 우발채무·특수관계자 주석, 현금흐름표)를 가장 먼저 읽고, 경영진의 설명(실적 발표문, 콘퍼런스콜, 셀사이드)을 가장 나중에 읽으며, 모든 설명을 "빼기(subtraction)·반전(reversal)·독립 계열(independent series)"의 세 검증에 통과시킨 뒤에야 모순이라고 부른다.** Chanos는 "start first with the SEC filings, then go to press releases, then go to earnings calls and other research. Work your way out."이라 가르치고, Clapham은 "I start at the back and I look at the contingencies, note, I look at the related parties note, I look at the audit report and then I go through the balance sheet."라고 말하며, Schilit의 첫 테스트는 2002년 2판 이래 영업현금흐름 대 순이익이다. 비율은 **기말 잔액 ÷ 당기 유량(분기 × 91.25, 연 × 365)**으로 계산하고 자기 과거 대비 YoY로 읽으며, 공개된 수치 임계값은 Beneish(−1.78/−1.49), Dechow F-score(1.00/1.85/2.45), GMT Research(동종 80 백분위, OCF < 조정 현금이익 20%가 2년 연속), S&P 유동성(A/B ≥ 1.2x/1.5x/2x, EBITDA −15/−30/−50% 스트레스) 정도이고 나머지는 모두 "자기 과거 대비"라는 실무 휴리스틱이다. 경영진 설명은 PCAOB AS 2810.08의 네 가지 결함(implausible / inconsistent / imprecise / insufficient detail)으로 등급을 매기며, SEC 집행 기록(Under Armour, Elanco, Network Associates)은 "timing of shipments" 류의 설명이 바로 풀포워드와 채널 스터핑에 선행했음을 보여준다. 2023–2026년에는 ASU 2022-04(공급자금융 롤포워드), 2023-07(세그먼트 유의적 비용), 2023-09(관할권별 납부세액), 2023-08(가상자산 공정가치), 2024-03(비용 분해)이 새 XBRL 태그를 만들었고, SEC 회계집행은 FY2023 86건에서 FY2025 47건(Cornerstone AAER 기준 2025년 10건)으로 급감하며 통제 단독 사건에서 스캔터 기반 사기 사건으로 이동했다. 학술 연구는 모든 스크린이 거짓양성 대 진양성 100:1 이상임을 보여, 스크린 플래그는 결코 단독으로 모순이 될 수 없음을 확인한다.

## 읽는 순서는 섹션이 아니라 정보원의 편향으로 정해진다

실무자가 공개적으로 밝힌 읽기 순서는 모두 같은 원리를 따른다. **덜 관리된 정보를 먼저, 더 관리된 정보를 나중에.** Chanos는 이유를 이렇게 설명한다: "This way you are looking at the most unbiased sources first. People on earnings calls will try and spin things, and analyst reports will obviously have a point of view. All of that is fine, because hopefully you will have first read the unvarnished facts." 어느 필링부터 읽느냐는 질문에는 "Yes, look for language changes. Read the most current ones and work your way backwards. Read the proxy statements that are often neglected and are full of great information. By doing that and by spending a night or two with those documents, you can have a remarkably comprehensive view about a company."라고 답했다 ([Graham & Doddsville 2012, Acquirer's Multiple 재게재](https://acquirersmultiple.com/?p=4088)). Kynikos 분석가들의 순서를 Katsenelson은 "His analysts always start research with a company's SEC filings, then listen to the company's conference calls and presentations, and only at the very, very end do they read outside research. As Jim put it, 'It's like peeling the onion from inside out'."라고 전한다 ([GuruFocus, VALUEx Vail 2012](https://www.gurufocus.com/news/184399/jim-chanos-the-warren-buffett-of-short-selling-valuex-vail-notes-2012)).

Stephen Clapham(전 Tiger cub 파트너, Behind the Balance Sheet)은 페이지 순서를 가장 구체적으로 말한 실무자다. 그가 뒤에서부터 여는 이유는 손익계산서는 이미 알고 있기 때문이다: "Most people open the accounts and look at the P&L ... usually by the time I've got to the accounts already familiar with the P&L ... So what I'm trying to do when I first opened the accounts is get a sense of whether there's a risk that accounts are crooked or not." 그의 세 가지 첫 점검은 순서가 정해져 있다: "my first check is look at the margins ... if the margins are higher than the peer group, then I ask myself, why is that? ... My second check is to ask myself, are their earnings turning into cash? ... And the third test is looking at working capital ratios. ... if the company is resorting to earnings management, it usually shows off in the working capital." 마진을 첫째로 둔 근거는 경험적이다: "I looked at every past fraud I could find. And the one thing that stood out about most of the frauds, not all ... the vast majority of frauds had margins that were odd" ([Compounding Curiosity ep. 22, 2021년 12월](https://compoundingcuriosity.substack.com/p/ep22)). 이 세 점검은 Schilit의 첫 테스트와 겹친다. Schilit: "Going back to the second edition in 2002, I said a good way for investors to see whether there was manipulation in the earnings would be to compare the earnings with the cash flow from operations." ([IR Magazine Q&A](https://irmagazine.com/reporting/shenanigans-qa)). 그 추세 버전은 공동 움직임의 단절을 본다: "If a company's change in cash flow historically moves in tandem with the change in net income, then any sudden deviation (such as an increase in net income and a decrease in cash) may be cause for concern." ([Knowledge at Wharton](https://knowledge.wharton.upenn.edu/article/looking-for-evidence-of-accounting-chicanery-try-digging-deeper/)).

CFRA, GMT Research, Behind the Numbers는 페이지 순서 대신 **스크린 → 정독**의 2단계 파이프라인을 기술한다. CFRA: "Our process begins with actively monitoring a global universe of companies with a market cap over $1 billion for poor quality of reported financial results, weak operational metrics, or corporate governance problems." → "Hazard List" → "Biggest Concerns"; 정성 커버리지는 140여 개 기업, 정량 평가는 25,000여 개 기업이다 ([CFRA Forensic Accounting Research](https://www.cfraresearch.com/solutions/forensic-research/)). CFRA 방법론 문서는 운영 트리거가 절대 컷오프가 아니라 **십분위 순위**임을 밝힌다: "Analysts are alerted when a covered company falls into the worst deciles on forensic accounting quality scores, prompting deeper review" ([CFRA methodology PDF, Julius Baer 호스팅](https://www.juliusbaer.com/fileadmin/legal/cfra-equity-research-methodology.pdf)). GMT: "GMT Research has developed an Accounting & Governance (A&G) Screen which scrutinises every line item within financial statements for anomalies. Our methodology uses a percentile scoring system for each financial ratio relative to a relevant industry peer group." ([GMT Accounting Screen](https://www.gmtresearch.com/en/accounting-screen/)). Behind the Numbers는 스크린의 한계를 분명히 한다: "Simple 'red flag' ratio analysis is merely a jumping off point. In our experience, such screens produce as many false positives as they do false negatives." ([BTN Research process](https://btnresearch.com/quality-of-earnings-analysis)).

### 첫날의 교차 점검: 발표문 대 10-Q, 현금흐름표 대 재무상태표

8-K Item 2.02 실적 발표문을 10-Q/10-K와 라인별로 대조하는 이유는 발표문이 "filed"가 아니라 "furnished"이고 감사 검토를 받지 않기 때문이다: "earnings releases are furnished to the SEC rather than filed as they are with the quarterly report, which means the company faces less liability for misstatement in an earnings release. And while audit firms review disclosures in the 10-Q (and audit them for the 10-K), audit firms don't need to examine disclosures in the earnings release at all." Calcbench가 찾은 사례는 **매출은 그대로인데 세금·소송 라인에서 순이익이 바뀐** 경우다: Virtu의 2017년 순이익은 발표문 $17.3m → 10-K $2.9m, AmerisourceBergen은 $414.5m → $364.5m(소송합의금 $864.4m → $914.4m) ([Radical Compliance, 2019년 2월](https://www.radicalcompliance.com/2019/02/25/discrepancies-corporate-disclosure)). 대조가 가능한 시간차는 평균 3.4일이다: "The average lag time between the filing of the 8-K and the filing of the 10-Q, was 3.4 days in 2018, and 3.7 days in 2017"; 발표문 작성 주체도 갈린다: "52% of the respondents to this survey said that the earnings announcement is prepared by the accounting department; 47% said the investor relations department was responsible for it." ([XBRL US, SEC 의견서 2019년 3월](https://sec.gov/comments/s7-26-18/s72618-5177727-183527.pdf)). 발표문 이후 10-Q 전에 수정된 최근 사례로는 Sunlight Financial(2023년 5월, 중단사업 손실 재분류), Clover Health(2021년 4월 8-K/A: 파생상품 평가손익 조정으로 순손실 $(81.6)m → $(126.4)m, "management determined that there was a material weakness"), Lennar(2017년, 소송 충당으로 분기 순이익 $130.8m → $38.1m)가 있다 ([Sunlight EX-99.1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1762322/000176232223000034/sft2023331-ex991xdiscopslo.htm); [Clover 8-K/A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1801170/000119312521102873/d118254d8ka.htm); [Lennar EX-99.1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/920760/000162828017003494/bevardex991-2017228x8xkq1.htm)). 발견된 모든 사례에서 수정 방향은 발표문에 **불리한** 쪽이었다.

현금흐름표를 재무상태표 변동과 스스로 다시 맞추는 이유는 SEC 수석회계사가 직접 말했다: "the statement of cash flows consistently being a leading area of financial statement restatements"이며 "cash flow information is often used as a proxy to understand earnings quality." 분류 오류를 사소하다고 보는 논리는 거부된다: "In certain instances, the staff in OCA have been presented with analyses that conclude an error in the statement of cash flows is not material because it is an error in classification only. We have not found such analyses and their corresponding arguments persuasive since classification itself is the foundation of the statement of cash flows." ([Munter, 2023년 12월 4일](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/munter-statement-cash-flows-120423)). Schilit은 세 섹션 사이의 이동을 본다: "So here's the plan: you move all the good stuff into operating cash flow, and anything that is going to be a minus to cash flow you move into the second or third section (investing or financing)." ([IR Magazine](https://irmagazine.com/reporting/shenanigans-qa)). 운전자본 라인이 재무상태표 변동과 다른 정당한 이유는 "finite and knowable"하다: 비현금 취득·처분, 사업 인수·매각, 외화환산, 유동/비유동 재분류, 대손상각·재고평가손 같은 비현금 상각 등이며, 설명되지 않는 잔차는 "UNEXPLAINED — DO NOT PLUG"로 남겨야 한다 ([cpa-skills cash-flow-tieout](https://github.com/adoptai/cpa-skills/blob/dev/markdown/cash-flow-tieout.md)). Mulford는 인수가 CFO를 부풀리는 경로를 지적한다: "cash paid to acquire net working capital accounts, including accounts receivable, inventory and accounts payable, is reported in the investing section ... The ultimate realization through sale or collection of these accounts boosts operating cash flow. No disclosures are provided of the extent to which acquisitions have boosted operating cash flow." ([NACM, Creative Cash Flow Reporting](https://nacm.org/pdfs/gscfm/%36.FW%36._Creative_Cash_Flow.pdf)). 어느 실무자 자료도 이 대사(tie-out)의 산술을 문장으로 적어두지는 않았다 — 이는 메모 전체에서 확인된 공백이다.

### 조용한 변화: 언어 diff, little r, 사라진 KPI, CAM

Chanos에게 언어 변화는 1급 신호다: "I am a big believer in looking for changes in language in a company's filings over time. During the year we were short Enron, each successive filing had incrementally more damning disclosure about the company's offbalance sheet entities. ... Language changes are not accidental. They are argued over internally." ([Acquirer's Multiple](https://acquirersmultiple.com/?p=4088)). Carson Block도 같은 절차를 콜 트랜스크립트에 적용한다: "we'll typically print off three or four years worth of transcripts, and see how management's language has evolved. If there are sudden changes in topics, language or key performance indicators (KPI), then that could also be suspicious." ([MoneyWeek, 2025년 6월](https://moneyweek.com/investments/stocks-and-shares/carson-block-short-selling)). 2025년 툴체인은 이 작업을 원클릭으로 만들었다: "Blacklining (also known as 'redlining') allows you to compare changes between the latest version and the prior period's version of a company's SEC filings (proxy documents, 10Ks and 10Qs)." ([AlphaSense Help, 2025년 9월](https://help.alpha-sense.com/hc/en-us/articles/41877819090579-Track-Changes-Between-Major-Filings-with-Blacklining)).

조용한 정정의 주 경로는 "little r" 수정이다. SEC 정의: "If the error is not material to previously-issued financial statements, but either correcting the error or leaving the error uncorrected would be material to the current period financial statements, a registrant must still correct the error, but is not precluded from doing so in the current period comparative financial statements by restating the prior period information and disclosing the error." 비중은 **2005년 약 35% → 2020년 약 76%**로 늘었고, SEC는 편향을 경고한다: "we have observed that some materiality analyses appear to be biased toward supporting an outcome that an error is not material to previously-issued financial statements, resulting in 'little r' revision restatements." ([Munter, 2022년 3월 9일](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/munter-statement-assessing-materiality-030922)). 현금흐름표 오류는 대부분 little r로 처리된다: "a significant majority of these restatements represent prior period errors corrected in the current period comparative financial statements" ([Munter, 2023년 12월](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/munter-statement-cash-flows-120423)). CAQ 집계로 2013–2022년 재작성 5,793건 중 Big R 1,352건, little r 4,441건이다 ([Mayer Brown, CAQ 보고서 요약](https://acrosstheboard.mayerbrown.com/2024/06/caq-report-on-financial-statement-restatement-trends/)). Audit Analytics는 8-K Item 4.02 날짜 필드로 둘을 가른다: "If there's a date, it's a Big R; if blank, it's a little r." ([Audit Analytics help](https://auditanalytics.help.ideagen.com/hc/en-gb/articles/35432233953554/)). little r은 4.02 8-K를 만들지 않으므로, 실무적 탐지는 현재 필링의 전기 비교 열을 원래 보고된 숫자와 숫자로 diff하는 것이다 [INFERENCE]. 한편 기관투자자의 **78%가 CAM을 "always/often" 읽고** 92%가 투자 결정에 사용하며, 의사결정에 영향을 준 CAM 유형은 자산·영업권 평가 22%, 재무제표 오류 21%, 수익인식 18%, 경영진 추정 12%였다 ([CAQ/KRC CAM 설문, 2024년 Q3](https://www.thecaq.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/caq_cams-survey-research-findings-q3-survey_2024-07.pdf)). Behind the Numbers는 이 작업이 데이터베이스로 대체되지 않는 이유를 말한다: "It requires constant monitoring of footnotes and supplemental disclosures looking for changes that appear out of line with underlying trends. Much of this data is not maintained by financial databases which means investors relying totally on quantitative approaches are likely not incorporating all of it into their models." ([Peek Behind the Numbers 소개](https://peekbehindthenumbers.substack.com/p/an-introduction-to-peek-behind-the)).

### 자기 과거 대 동종: 발생액은 자기 과거, 수준은 동종

이익의 질 신호는 **자기 과거**가 지배한다. GMT의 OCF 모델은 2년 지속을 요구한다: "The model will flash red each year that a company reports operating cash flow less than 20% of adjusted cash earnings but the model is only triggered when operating cash flow is low for two consecutive years. If triggered, a working capital analysis should be conducted to determine if the shortfall relates to a deterioration in terms of trade, aggressive revenue recognition or something else." ([GMT OpCF reconciliation](https://www.gmtresearch.com/en/accounting-ratio/opcf-accruals/)). 동종은 **수준의 개연성**(Clapham의 마진, GMT의 GICS 백분위)에 쓰인다. 다만 Chanos는 자기 과거를 가치평가의 기준점으로 삼는 것을 경계한다: value investors "look at a company's past earnings (and/or cash flows), and that becomes our reference point. But the value of any asset is the present value of its future – not past – cash flows." ([GuruFocus](https://www.gurufocus.com/news/184399/jim-chanos-the-warren-buffett-of-short-selling-valuex-vail-notes-2012)). 어떤 포렌식 기관도 5년 창이나 TTM/동분기 규칙을 명시적으로 공표하지 않았다는 점은 공백으로 남는다.

### 2023–2026년 툴체인: 넓고 싼 부분은 기계, 깊은 부분은 사람

구조화 데이터는 "모든 라인, 모든 기업, 모든 기간"을 API로 옮겼지만 SEC 자체가 태그 결함 목록을 유지한다: "Cash Flow Statement Data Element Presentation — March 8, 2024", "Incorrect Tagging for Earnings Per Share Data — May 30, 2024", "Changing Labels for the Same Reported Item on the Income Statement over Multiple Periods — November 3, 2023" 등 ([SEC ODSI Staff Observations](https://www.sec.gov/data-research/structured-data/office-data-standards-Innovation-staff-observations-guidance-trends)). 태그 안정성 점검 없이 추세 산술을 하면 태깅의 조용한 변화가 사업의 조용한 변화처럼 보인다 [INFERENCE]. LLM은 2024–2025년에 분류·diff·요약에 쓰이며, 벤더 스스로 수치 계산이 약점임을 인정한다: "Natural language summaries: 91% accurate with source verification. Data extraction to Excel: 88% accurate for standard formats. Numerical calculations: 52% accurate (this is why you never trust LLM math)"; "The golden rule: Every number must link back to a source document" ([Daloopa, 2024년 9월](https://daloopa.com/blog/analyst-best-practices/exploratory-financial-data-analysis-using-large-language-models)). 2025년 포렌식 레시피는 위험을 직접 말한다: "A forensic short pass is exactly where vanilla LLMs are most dangerous. The work hinges on specific, recent, cross-referenced numbers — operating cash flow vs. net income over three years, the diluted share-count trend, the days-sales-outstanding creep, the 8-K item codes around a CFO departure. Base models invent these" 그리고 "Every dollar amount, percentage, date, and quoted phrase must come from a tool result tied to an accession number. If a value isn't in the filings, write UNKNOWN — never approximate." 그 레시피의 결과물은 심각도 순 "RED-FLAG INVENTORY", 2–3개 베어 테제, "WHAT WOULD DISPROVE THE SHORT (falsifiers)"이며, "'No fraud-class red flags identified' is a valid — and common — result on a healthy company." ([edgar.tools Short-Seller Forensics](https://app.edgar.tools/mcp/recipes/short_seller_forensics)). 어떤 이름 있는 포렌식 기관(CFRA, BTN, GMT, Schilit Forensics)도 LLM 사용법을 공표하지 않았으므로 AI 워크플로 자료는 전부 벤더 문서로 읽어야 한다.

## 비율은 기말 잔액으로, 임계값은 공개 규칙과 휴리스틱을 구분해서

### 운전자본 일수와 성장률 비교

포렌식 용도의 DSO/DSI/DPO는 **평균이 아니라 기말 잔액**을 쓴다. Schilit: "For financial shenanigan detection purposes, we recommend calculating DSI using the ending balance of inventory (rather than the average balance, as some companies and texts may advise). The ending balance provides more relevant data about future margin pressure, potential obsolescence, product demand, and so on." Coldwater Creek 사례에서 재고 증가 67.2%를 경영진의 **기대** 매출 성장 24.3%와 비교했다 ([Financial Shenanigans 3판 발췌](https://read-online-books.com/book/read-online-financial-shenanigans-how-to-detect-accounting-gimmicks-fraud-in-financial-reports-3rd-edition-free/page-21)). Kerrisdale은 각주로 정의를 못박는다: "Days sales outstanding calculated as most recent quarter-end receivables / quarterly revenue * (365/4), and is not average DSO." C%33.ai에서 DSO 197일, 미청구채권 <$10m → $88m, 4분기 누적 현금흐름표 매출채권 변동 −$76m 대 LTM 매출 $267m을 플래그했다 ([Kerrisdale, C%33.ai 감사인 서한, 2023](https://www.kerrisdalecap.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/C%33.ai-Auditor-Note.pdf)). SEC 스태프도 코멘트레터에서 최근 분기 기준을 쓴다: "your day's sales outstanding, calculated based on the most recent quarter's sales, increased from 37.0 days at both December 31, 2012 and 2011 to 49.2 days at June 30, 2013." ([SEC 코멘트레터](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1232524/000000000013057994/filename1.pdf)). 팩토링된 채권은 **더해서** 계산한다. BTN(Coty, 2026년 1월): "When we include factored receivables that COTY removes from the balance sheet, we see that payment cycles have extended by 19 days over the last two years", 그리고 일수를 매출·EPS로 환산한다("one day worth $17 million in Q1 ... This translates to 2.5 cents of EPS benefit"); Abbott는 역사적 평균 약 55일에서 65.6일로, "Each day of receivables represents approximately $123 million in sales." ([BTN Substack, 2026년 1월](https://peekbehindthenumbers.substack.com/p/accounting-and-earnings-quality-signals)). Schilit은 "Take notice when a company sells its accounts receivable, or turns accounts receivable into notes receivable or even other parts of the balance sheet (e.g. Cash equivalent). Be sure to adjust for these moves in calculating Days in Receivable or other related metrics."라고 쓴다 ([Scribd, Financial Shenanigans 노트](https://www.scribd.com/document/465460323/Financial-Shenanigans)). CFRA는 Velti에서 회사가 매출채권만 넣어 DSO를 86일로 보고한 반면, 기타·미청구채권을 포함하면 128일 → 184일임을 보였다 ([CFRA 2014 덱](https://studylib.net/doc/8769790/financial-shenanigans-europe-oct-2014)).

실무자가 밝힌 크기의 휴리스틱은 모두 소프트하다. Del Vecchio & Jacobs: "even just a few days year-over-year increase in DSO can signal poor earnings quality and potential trouble ahead" [PRACTITIONER HEURISTIC] ([einvestingforbeginners](https://einvestingforbeginners.com/calculating-dso-formula-examples/)). BTN: "A year-over-year increase in DSO of two to three days could be a sign a company is pulling revenue into the current quarter at the expense of the next. Or it could simply be a matter of the timing of collections." ([Peek Behind the Numbers, 2021년 11월](https://peekbehindthenumbers.substack.com/p/revenue-recognition-and-accounts-964)). "AR growing faster than revenue for 2+ years", "CFFO / Net Income ratio. Below 1.0 for multiple years", "Negative FCF for 3+ years while reporting profits", "Goodwill + Intangibles as % of Shareholder Equity. Above 40%", "Debt/EBITDA ... Above 4x is stress territory. Above 6x is distress."는 모두 [PRACTITIONER HEURISTIC]이다 ([EarningsGrade](https://earningsgrade.com/methodology)). "DSO up two quarters running"은 어느 출처에서도 확인되지 않았고 Schilit의 표현은 "several quarters of growing receivables"다. 역방향도 신호다: Schilit의 체크리스트에는 "Revenue growing much faster than accounts receivable"(가공 매출), "A huge decline in DSO following several quarters of growing receivables", "Inappropriate or changing methods of calculating DSO"가 들어 있다 ([Scribd](https://www.scribd.com/document/465460323/Financial-Shenanigans)). 2024년 감사인 벤치마크 연구는 고전적 DSO 스크린에 불리하다: "the difference between a company's revenue growth and the revenue growth of its industry has historically been the best fraud indicator ... for fraud firms, revenue growth is 17.2 percent greater than industry revenue growth. The same difference for nonfraud firms is only 4.8 percent"인 반면 "the differences between revenue growth and both growth in accounts receivable and growth in cost of goods sold are not significant between fraud firms and the nonfraud sample." ([Brazel et al. 2024](https://www.newswise.com/pdf_docs/172467885077957_Brazel%20benchmarks%20and%20fraud%20risk%202024%20FINAL.pdf)).

### 주석에서 다시 쌓는 롤포워드와 거기서 읽는 플래그

US GAAP과 Reg S-X가 롤포워드를 의무화한 계정은 대손충당금(ASC 326-20-50-13: "Beginning balance ... Current-period provision ... Write-offs ... Recoveries ... Ending balance"), 제품보증(ASC 460-10-50-8), 영업권(ASC 350-20-50-1, 총액과 누적손상을 세그먼트별로), 구조조정부채(ASC 420), 계약부채(ASC 606-10-50-8/50-10: 기초 계약부채에서 인식한 수익), 그리고 2024년부터 공급자금융 의무(ASC 405-50-50-3)다 ([LegalClarity](https://legalclarity.org/accounts-receivable-disclosure-example-and-requirements/); [PwC Viewpoint 8.8](https://viewpoint.pwc.com/dt/us/en/pwc/accounting_guides/financial_statement_/financial_statement___18_US/chapter_8_other_asse_US/8_8_goodwill.html); [PwC/AICPA Contract Balances](https://viewpoint.pwc.com/dt/us/en/aicpav2/aag-con/aag-con/aag-con2/aag-con2_other_related_topics/aag-con2_disclosures_and_presentation/aag-con2-disclosures-and-presentationn-d19978aAaB.html); [PwC ASU 2022-04](https://viewpoint.pwc.com/dt/us/en/fasb_financial_accou/asus_fulltext/2022/asu2022-04/ASU%32022-04/liabilitiessuppfinprog.html)). 10-K의 Schedule II(S-X 12-09)는 "Balance at beginning of period | Additions (1) Charged to costs and expenses (2) Charged to other accounts – describe | Deductions – describe | Balance at end of period" 열로 모든 평가성 계정을 담는다 ([PwC Viewpoint S-X 12-09](https://viewpoint.pwc.com/dt/us/en/sec/regulations/regulation_sx/regulation_sx_US/article_12_form_and___1_US/general__12_US/rule_1209_valuation___1_US.html)).

롤포워드에서 읽는 플래그는 네 종류다. 첫째, **적립 < 사용**: 감사인 기법으로 "it is reasonable to expect the ratio of bad debt expense to write-offs to be close to 1.0 over an extended period", 기초충당금 ÷ 상각액은 "benchmark figure of one to two years", 그리고 "a company might opportunistically reduce the allowance in a period of reduced earnings." [PRACTITIONER HEURISTIC] ([Journal of Accountancy 2009](https://www.journalofaccountancy.com/issues/2009/sep/20091539/)). 둘째, **총액 대비 충당 비율의 하락**: "Bad debt reserves decline relative to gross receivables: Potential misclassification of receivables or significant write-offs of receivables; potential understatement of reserves." ([Decker & Kizirian 2017](https://doi.org/10.19030/jbcs.v13i4.10032)). 셋째, **전기 판매분 추정 변경**: Ford 2024 보증 롤포워드에서 "$2.7 billion of the 2024 expense, and $2.6 billion in 2023, came from raising estimates on vehicles sold in earlier periods, a sign that past reserves had been too low" ([IB Interview Questions reserves guide](https://ibinterviewquestions.com/guides/accounting-investment-banking/reserves-and-allowances-bad-debt-returns-warranties)). 넷째, **이익으로의 환입**: Schilit의 "When all reserve accruals are moving in the wrong direction (i.e., declining), head for the hills!" ([Financial Shenanigans 4판 리뷰](https://linhth.com/2021/02/12/financial-shenanigans-fourth-edition-howard-schilit/))와 "Creating acquisition-related reserves and releasing them into income in a later period" ([Scribd](https://www.scribd.com/document/465460323/Financial-Shenanigans)). 영업권은 SEC FRM 9510.3이 위험 보고단위에 대해 "The percentage by which fair value exceeded carrying value as of the date of the most recent test; The amount of goodwill allocated to the reporting unit; A description of the methods and key assumptions"를 기대하므로, 그 **헤드룸 퍼센트**와 그 부재가 플래그다 ([SEC FRM Topic 9](https://www.sec.gov/corpfin/cf-manual/topic-9)). 어느 실무자도 충당금/총매출채권, 보증충당/매출, 재고충당/재고의 수치 임계값을 말하지 않는다 — 전부 자기 과거나 동종 대비다. 일반 항등식 기초 + 비용처리 추가 + 기타 − 사용(상각·환입) = 기말로부터 적립÷사용, 기말충당÷총액, 커버리지 연수, 전기추정변경÷당기비용을 계산할 수 있고, 이연수익은 Δ계약부채 대 Δ매출, 빌링 프록시 = 매출 + Δ이연수익, 기초잔액 중 인식 수익÷기초잔액이 계산 가능하다 [INFERENCE].

### 비GAAP 대조: SEC C&DI가 정한 세 가지 결함

2022년 12월 13일 C&DI는 회사 측 조정의 판정 기준이다. Q%3100.01: "Presenting a non-GAAP performance measure that excludes normal, recurring, cash operating expenses necessary to operate a registrant's business is one example of a measure that could be misleading ... The staff would view an operating expense that occurs repeatedly or occasionally, including at irregular intervals, as recurring." Q%3102.10(a)의 과도한 강조 예: "Presenting a non-GAAP measure before the most directly comparable GAAP measure or omitting the comparable GAAP measure altogether, including in an earnings release headline"; Q%3102.10(b): "Starting the reconciliation with a non-GAAP measure"; "Presenting a non-GAAP income statement" ([SEC Non-GAAP C&DIs](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/staff-guidance/corporation-finance-interpretations/non-gaap-financial-measures)). Q%3100.04의 개별맞춤 회계 예: "changing the pattern of recognition, such as including an adjustment ... to accelerate revenue recognized ratably over time ... as though revenue was earned when customers were billed" ([Ropes & Gray](https://www.ropesgray.com/en/insights/alerts/2022/12/sec-staff-updates-guidance-on-disclosure-of-non-gaap-financial-measures)). 2024년 12월의 실제 코멘트는 이렇게 적용한다: "the amounts related to Inventory write-off and RTD liquidation appear to be normal, recurring operating expenses ... Refer to both Questions 100.01 and 100.04"; "remove the characterization 'nonrecurring' from non-GAAP adjustments that occur in multiple periods." ([SEC CORRESP, 2024년 12월](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1891101/000114036124048401/filename1.htm)). 실무자 측 질문은 BTN의 것이다: "what if it's an expense that has been going on for 15 years such as stock compensation, amortization of intangible assets, or 'one-time' restructuring charges that never end? At what point does one question it?" ([Peek Behind the Numbers 소개](https://peekbehindthenumbers.substack.com/p/an-introduction-to-peek-behind-the)). Chanos(2023): "its mind-blowing now on how corporations report their results and what they exclude and what they want you to exclude" ([Buteau 편집, Meb Faber Show 479](https://www.antoinebuteau.com/lessons-from-jim-chanos/)). 계산 가능한 테스트는 조정이익÷GAAP이익과 그 추세, 조정 범주의 수와 재발(2기 이상 등장하면 "nonrecurring" 라벨 실패), SBC·구조조정·소송·상각 제외 여부, 대조가 GAAP에서 출발하는지 여부다 [INFERENCE].

### SBC, 공급자금융, 팩토링, 리스를 현금흐름과 레버리지에 넣는 법

신용평가사가 정확한 규칙을 공표한다. S&P는 ASC 842 운용리스를 조정부채에 넣고 비용을 재분류한다: "Debt: We include the reported amount of lease obligations in adjusted debt. Income statement and cash flow measures: The reported operating lease cost is allocated to interest and depreciation expenses. EBITDA is increased by adding back the interest and depreciation expenses ... CFO is increased by adding back the depreciation expense ... Interest expense is increased by multiplying the average operating lease obligation ... by the reported weighted average operating lease discount rate." 채권 매각은 비소구라도 차입이다: "We regard the securitization, sale, and factoring of trade receivables ... as being akin to secured financing. We make this adjustment even when the transaction is non-recourse ... Debt: Add the amount of period-end securitized assets to reported debt. CFO: Reverse the impact of cash flow movements from the initiation of a securitization, subsequent changes in amounts securitized, or the securitization's maturity." ([S&P Ratios and Adjustments](https://spratings.spglobal.com/ratings/en/regulatory/article/-/view/sourceId/10906146)). Moody's: "We reclassify lease liabilities to debt."; 현금흐름은 "we reclassify both operating lease depreciation expense (from operating activities) and lease principal repayments (from financing activities) to capital [expenditures]"; 채권 매각에 대해 "we see little analytical difference between sale/securitization and collateralized borrowing" ([Moody's FSA 방법론](https://ratings.moodys.com/api/rmc-documents/69913); [Footnotes Analyst 비교](https://www.footnotesanalyst.com/leasing-and-leverage-credit-rating-agencies-disagree/)). 공급자금융은 두 기관이 갈린다: "Moody's views 90 days as a 'reasonable upper limit' for classifying such an exposure as a trade payable ... S&P Global Ratings, however, treats trade payables that remain outstanding after 90 days under a supplier finance arrangement as a form of borrowing." 2025년 9월 Moody's 표본에서 121개사 중 47개사가 평균 90일 초과였다 ([GTR, 2025년 9월](https://www.gtreview.com/news/americas/moodys-warns-over-unreasonable-supply-chain-finance-payment-terms/)). Footnotes Analyst는 현금흐름 총액화를 제안한다: "an operating outflow when the bank pays the supplier with a financing inflow on the same date. When the supplier later pays the bank, this would be a financing outflow." ([Footnotes Analyst](https://www.footnotesanalyst.com/leverage-and-cash-flow-effects-of-supply-chain-finance/)). 새 공시로 드러난 규모는 Bloomberg Tax "$64 Billion of Hidden Leverage at Big US Firms"(2023년 5월), S&P "$85 Billion Of U.S. Supplier Finance Obligations Come To Light"(2023년 7월)이다 ([Bloomberg Tax](https://news.bloombergtax.com/financial-accounting/new-rules-reveal-64-billion-of-hidden-leverage-at-big-us-firms); [S&P](https://www.spglobal.com/ratings/en/regulatory/article/-/view/sourceId/12771991)). 평가사 규칙을 따른 조정부채 = 보고부채 + 운용리스부채 + 기말 매각·유동화 채권 + 정상 조건을 넘는 공급자금융 의무(S&P: >90일); 조정 CFO = 보고 CFO − Δ매각채권 − Δ공급자금융 의무다 [INFERENCE]. SBC는 Larson·Sloan·Zha Giedt(2018)가 발생액에서 제외하는 이유를 밝힌다: "The earnings adjustment for stock-based compensation expense essentially involves a transfer between paid-in capital and retained earnings, with no net effect on common equity ... we exclude this earnings adjustment from our comprehensive definition and measure of accruals." ([RAST 2018](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11142-018-9457-z)). 포렌식 테스트는 SBC÷매출과 GAAP–비GAAP 영업이익 차이 중 SBC 귀속분이다.

## 방향은 질문을 만들고, 크기·반전·독립 계열이 모순을 만든다

### 공매도자의 세 가지 어조와 입증 기준

공개 보고서는 세 어조를 쓴다. 법적 보일러플레이트("This report is opinion journalism" — [Muddy Waters, Ensign 2026년 6월](https://muddywatersresearch.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/MW_ENSG_20260611.pdf)), 결론 어조, 질문 어조다. 입증 기준은 민사다. Chanos: "Investing is like a civil trial. You need a preponderance of evidence, not beyond a reasonable doubt." ([Acquirer's Multiple](https://acquirersmultiple.com/2016/08/how-to-succeed-in-investing-and-why-you-shouldnt-listen-to-analysts-james-chanos/)). Block: "We have to be provably correct at the time we speak about our theses." 그리고 회색지대: "More commonly, our shorts exhibit a large amount of hype that is highly misleading but not legally fraudulent. This grey zone is the biggest problem for investors and markets." ([MoneyWeek 2025](https://moneyweek.com/investments/stocks-and-shares/carson-block-short-selling)). 기준이 과거지향인 이유도 Block이 말한다: "we want things that are provable and that we can prove now and that's basically… something that has to have already happen[ed] so we're looking backward" ([Real Vision 2019 트랜스크립트](https://rosetta.to/u/realvisionfinance/exposing-jack-ma-big-pharma-and-chinese-fraud-w-carson-block)). 단일 소스 일화는 증거가 아니다: "if you were to go public and say, 'I spoke to a former employee of IQiyi or XYZ Company, and that's why I think it's a fraud,' then that would be very, very poorly sourced and supported." 그리고 크기 기준: "it's well north of that 30% bar that we want to clear." ([Kinetic Energy Ventures 인터뷰, 2020](https://12mv2.com/2020/04/21/carson-block-interview-4-15/)). 결론 어조가 허용되는 것은 문서화된 사실이 여러 개 모일 때다: Muddy Waters는 "We sent investigators to 57 of Ensign's 379 facilities across eight states. We found red flags consistent with rented licenses at 12 of these facilities"와 전직 직원 8명을 제시하고도 "We believe this scheme, which could amount to fraud"라고 헤지한다 ([Muddy Waters ENSG](https://muddywatersresearch.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/MW_ENSG_20260611.pdf)). Hindenburg의 Adani 보고서는 질문 어조를 점수표로 바꿨다: 88개 질문 중 "Adani failed to specifically answer 62 of them. Instead, it mainly grouped questions together in categories and provided generalized deflections. In other instances, Adani simply pointed to its own filings" ([Hindenburg, Adani 회신](https://hindenburgresearch.com/adani-response/)). **무응답 수 자체가 발견으로 보고된다** [INFERENCE]. Chanos는 경영진을 정보원에서 아예 제외한다: "We never meet with management." "The CFO will always have an answer for you as to why a certain number that looks odd really is normal, and why some development that looks negative is actually positive." ([Acquirer's Multiple](https://acquirersmultiple.com/2016/08/how-to-succeed-in-investing-and-why-you-shouldnt-listen-to-analysts-james-chanos/)). Schilit의 CFRA는 반대로 접촉을 문서화했다: "we spoke to at least 75 percent of the companies and we would make at least three separate overtures to them. We always wanted to document everything" ([IR Magazine](https://irmagazine.com/reporting/shenanigans-qa)).

### 경영진 설명을 검증하는 세 가지 테스트

실무 규칙은 경영진 설명을 **가설**로 두고 세 테스트를 건다. 첫째, **빼기 테스트**(크기): CFRA가 Software AG의 "one deal" 설명을 검증한 방식 — "Software AG told CFRA that the contract only accounted for €13 million. Excluding this item, DSO was up 21 days, suggesting the extension of payment terms." 둘째, **함의 매출 테스트**: BTN의 Dentsply Sirona — "if accounts receivable had grown in line with sales, sales would have been 1-5% lower than reported during the 2Q17-2Q18 quarters." 셋째, **반전 테스트**(시간): First Solar의 1Q09 10-Q는 "extended our customers' payment terms from 10 days to 45 days, net as well as the timing of shipments"라 했고, BTN은 "a year after the company extended the payment terms for its customers, the year-over-year growth in DSO stopped. Unfortunately, so did revenue growth which turned negative in 2Q10 despite an increase in DSO. This is a clue that demand had been pulled into the previous quarters"를 확인했다 ([CFRA 2014 덱](https://studylib.net/doc/8769790/financial-shenanigans-europe-oct-2014); [Peek Behind the Numbers, 2021년 11월](https://peekbehindthenumbers.substack.com/p/revenue-recognition-and-accounts-964)). 넷째로 **독립 계열**이 있다: "The fact that inventory levels at XRAY's largest customers were rising at the same time its receivables balances were creeping up was pretty strong evidence that XRAY was utilizing more attractive payment terms to entice customers to buy earlier than normal." BTN은 설명을 받아들이기 전에 맥락 질문을 덧붙인다: "What is the company's recent sales trend? If sales growth has been weakening or barely meeting forecasts for the last few quarters, it should increase the suspicion that the company is using unsustainable tactics" ([같은 글](https://peekbehindthenumbers.substack.com/p/revenue-recognition-and-accounts-964)). CFRA의 기본 입장: "When receivables rise relative to revenues, our concern is generally not one of receivable quality but rather of more aggressive revenue recognition." 그리고 짝짓기 규칙: "DSDR [days sales of deferred revenue] decline of greater concern when coupled with an increase in DSO."; "CFFO – NI is generally the first layer of screening for nonfinancial companies. Should a company fail this initial screen, we generally dig in deeper for the cause of the CFFO shortfall." ([CFRA 2014 덱](https://studylib.net/doc/8769790/financial-shenanigans-europe-oct-2014)).

집행 기록은 "timing/demand" 설명이 정확히 풀포워드에 선행했음을 보여준다. Under Armour의 2016년 설명은 "Receivables up due to timing of shipments. UAA shipped product to customers earlier than last year – so that those customers could set their floors for the consumer in time for the spring launch. DSO not expected to remain elevated in 2016."이었고 ([Argo Group 덱](https://www.argogroup.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/Strengthening-Your-Financial-Intelligence.pdf)), SEC 2021년 명령은 "For six consecutive quarters from the third quarter of 2015 through the fourth quarter of 2016… Under Armour used pull forwards to help it meet analysts' revenue estimates. During the Relevant Period, Under Armour pulled forward approximately $408 million in orders."라고 적시했다. 중요한 것은 SEC의 이론이 **허위 매출이 아니라 동인의 누락**이라는 점이다: "This Offer does not make any findings that revenue from these sales was not recorded in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles" ([SEC Order 33-10940](https://www.sec.gov/files/litigation/admin/2021/33-10940.pdf)). Elanco(2024년 11월)는 2020년 1분기 매출 10% 감소를 "more tight management of inventory by distributors and the impact of COVID"로 돌렸고, SEC는 "omitted the impact on sales of the decision to stop offering Quarter-End Incentivized Sales… and did not disclose that the plan to reduce channel inventory preceded the pandemic"라며 Reg S-K Item 303에 근거해 $15m을 부과했다 ([Cooley PubCo via Lexology](https://www.lexology.com/library/detail.aspx?g=de578d71-fdd9-48bf-9c75-3746d9db8576)). Network Associates에서는 DSO 자체가 관리 대상이었다: "Goyal and other senior executives' quarterly bonuses were tied to lowering the DSO." MD&A는 "we may from time to time take actions to encourage earlier payment of receivables and sell receivables"라고만 썼지만 "In fact, Network Associates sold receivables in every quarter in 1998…" ([SEC complaint 18748](https://www.sec.gov/files/litigation/complaints/comp18748.pdf)). 필링만 보는 분석가에게 유사체는 "설명이 거짓"이 아니라 **"설명이 크기에 비해 불완전"**이다 [INFERENCE].

### 감사기준이 주는 등급 어휘

PCAOB AS 2810.08은 설명의 네 결함을 이름 붙인다: "The auditor should obtain corroboration for management's explanations regarding significant unusual or unexpected transactions, events, amounts, or relationships. If management's responses to the auditor's inquiries appear to be implausible, inconsistent with other audit evidence, imprecise, or not at a sufficient level of detail to be useful, the auditor should perform procedures to address the matter." ([PCAOB AS 2810](https://pcaobus.org/oversight/standards/auditing-standards/details/AS%32810)). AS 2805.04: "If a representation made by management is contradicted by other audit evidence, the auditor should investigate the circumstances and consider the reliability of the representation made." ([PCAOB AS 2805](https://pcaobus.org/oversight/standards/auditing-standards/details/AS%32805)). 분석적 절차의 한계도 명시된다: "substantive analytical procedures alone are not well suited to detecting fraud"(AS 2305.10) ([PCAOB AS 2305](https://pcaobus.org/oversight/standards/auditing-standards/details/AS%32305)). 포렌식 실무 노트: "An indicator is not a finding." ([Forensic Accounting & Fraud Institute](https://forensicaccountinginstitute.com/detection-and-investigation/conducting-the-investigation/)).

SEC 코멘트레터는 "경영진에 대한 질문"의 공개 문법이다. Global Payments(2024년 10월 3일): "we note that cost of service decreased 1.4% despite a $0.67 billion, or 7.6%, increase in revenue." … "It would appear unlikely that you would be able to attribute a change in results to a factor while not having quantification of such factor. In addition, we are not aware of circumstances in which it would not be appropriate to provide quantification to investors of information that is available internally to, and used by, management." 상계도 금지된다: "the impacts of material variances in components of cost of service that offset each other should be separately disclosed, quantified, and discussed (and not netted)." ([SEC 스태프 서한, Global Payments IR 호스팅](https://investors.globalpayments.com/financial-information/all-sec-filings/content/0000000000-24-011174/0000000000-24-011174.pdf)). A. O. Smith(2026년 4월 21일): "Your MD&A attributes the 2025 effective tax rate change to reduced U.S. cross-border taxes, yet your rate reconciliation lacks a corresponding category."; Visa(2026년 3월 31일): "This factor appears to recur annually as the primary factor impacting your net revenue. Please discuss to the extent identifiable factors ... are the cause for the increased volume." ([SEC Reporting Desk 컴파일](https://secreportingdesk.com/topics/mdna)). SciClone(2007): "Disclose your standard credit terms and reconcile those terms to days' sales outstanding in accounts receivable if different." ([SciClone CORRESP](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000880771/000119312507205157/filename1.htm)). 스태프는 **정량화되지 않은 다요인 설명을 추정적으로 결함**으로 보며, "available internally to, and used by, management"를 정량화 가능성의 시험으로 삼는다 — 이것이 "필링으로 검증 불가능하지만 그럴듯한" 설명을 수용된 설명이 아니라 공시 결함으로 분류할 객관적 근거다 [INFERENCE].

### 왜 스크린 플래그는 단독으로 모순이 될 수 없는가

모든 출처가 수렴하는 규칙은 다음과 같다: (1) 주장과 반대 방향 → "질문"; (2) 정량화된 원인을 뺀 뒤에도 크기가 유의 → "설명 안 된 잔차"; (3) 예정된 반전이 일어나지 않거나 독립 계열(고객 재고, 이연수익, CFO)이 불일치 → "모순". 공매도자가 결론으로 공표하는 것은 (3)뿐이다 [INFERENCE]. 기저율이 이를 강제한다. Dechow의 0.0037은 "the unconditional probability that a company is a fraudulent reporter"이며, F-score 2.76은 "276% more likely to be a fraudulent reporter than a randomly selected company"일 뿐이다 ([OSU F-score 노트](https://cpb-us-w2.wpmucdn.com/u.osu.edu/dist/3/40936/files/2016/12/F-Score-xx23jg.pdf)). Beneish & Vorst: "even the best models trade off false to true positives at rates exceeding 100:1 ... For investors, M-Score and, at higher cut-offs, the F-Score, are the only models providing a net benefit"; 상위 5개 AUC 모델의 거짓양성률은 32%–58.8%이고, 투자자가 그래도 이득을 보는 이유는 "the firms that are falsely flagged by these models typically face significant headwind one-year-ahead in terms of both earnings and returns." ([Beneish & Vorst, Maastricht 저장소](https://cris.maastrichtuniversity.nl/ws/files/92114908/Vorst_2021_The_cost_of_fraud_prediction.pdf)). 사기를 실제로 밝히는 주체도 공매도자가 아니다: "Only 6% of the frauds are revealed by the SEC and 14% by the auditors. More important monitors are media (14%), industry regulators (16%), and employees (19%)." 다만 속도는 다르다: "Financial analysts and short sellers are in a league of their own, taking only a median duration of 9.1 months to reveal fraud" ([Dyck, Morse & Zingales, NBER w12882](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w12882/w12882.pdf)). 경영진 설명의 정보가치에 관한 학술 증거도 부정형이다: 콜에서 "about 11% of analyst questions elicit non-answers from managers" ([Gow, Larcker & Zakolyukina 2021](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/1475-679X.12371)); 기만적 콜의 언어 모델은 "significantly better than random by 4%-6%" ([Larcker & Zakolyukina 2012](https://ideas.repec.org/p/ecl/stabus/2060.html)); 투자자는 동종이 같은 문제를 공유하지 않을 때 자기변호적 귀인을 할인하며 그 할인은 사후적으로 정당화된다 — 최저 개연성 귀인 그룹 −5.11% 대 최고 그룹 −1.42% ([Kimbrough & Wang 2014](https://doi.org/10.2308/accr-50628); [Accounting Today](https://www.accountingtoday.com/news/investors-prove-wise-at-judging-self-serving-earnings-explanations)).

## 재무건전성: 정상화, 수익률 분해, 원천과 용도, 커버넌트, 자본배분

### FCF 정의 다섯 가지와 SBC·리스·인수의 처리

실무자는 최소 다섯 개의 FCF를 쓴다. Buffett의 owner earnings: "(a) reported earnings plus (b) depreciation, depletion, amortization, and certain other non-cash charges ... less ( c) the average annual amount of capitalized expenditures for plant and equipment, etc. that the business requires to fully maintain its long-term competitive position and its unit volume." EBITDA식 "cash flow"에 대한 경고: "These numbers routinely include (a) plus (b) - but do not subtract ( c) ." ([Berkshire 1986 주주서한](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/1986.html)). Damodaran: "the free cash flow to equity is the cash flow that a business generates after taxes, reinvestment and debt payments (interest and principal). The free cash flow to the firm is a pre-debt cash flow, before interest payments and debt repayments or issuances, but still after taxes and reinvestment."; SBC에 대해 "there is no justification for adding back stock-based compensation to get to cash flows, and none of the numerous variants of adjusted EBITDA that you see populating annual reports or prospectuses holds up to scrutiny."; 주식 인수는 재투자: "Microsoft bought Nuance Communications for $19.76 billion in all stock transaction, and that amount should be treated as reinvestment for the year, even though it is technically non-cash." R&D 자본화는 FCFE에 중립이다: "adds about $3.7 billion to net income, about $17 billion to depreciation and amortization and about $20.7 billion to cap ex, netting out to no effect on FCFE" ([Damodaran, 2022년 10월](https://aswathdamodaran.blogspot.com/2022/10/)). Mauboussin & Callahan: "FCF equals NOPAT minus the investment in future growth ... Investment in future growth is therefore equivalent to the change in invested capital." 그리고 CFO − capex 지름길 비판: "stock-based compensation is added back to calculate cash flow from operating activities. This means that a potentially significant expense is ignored. ... Finally, the common definition of FCF excludes investments made through lease commitments. These should be considered equivalent to capital expenditures but are recorded in cash flow from financing activities." ([Mauboussin & Callahan, ROIC 2022](https://www.eatonvance.com/content/dam/im/assets/publication/thought-leadership/consilient-observer/article_returnoninvestedcapital.pdf)). SBC 규모: "SBC was about $270 billion in 2022, or 6-8 percent of total compensation for public companies in the U.S."; "More than 95 percent of public companies now report non-GAAP results"; "Buybacks that offset dilution turn SBC, a non-cash expense, into a cash expense." ([Mauboussin & Callahan, SBC 2023](https://www.morganstanley.com/im/publication/insights/articles/article_stockbasedcompensation.pdf)). 평가사 정의: S&P "Free operating cash flow (FOCF): CFO minus capital expenditures", "Discretionary cash flow (DCF): Free operating cash flow (FOCF) minus cash dividends paid on common and preferred stock, less share buybacks", "Funds from operations (FFO): EBITDA, minus cash interest paid minus cash tax paid" ([S&P Ratios and Adjustments 2019, Maalot 사본](https://www.maalot.co.il/Publications/MT20190402125127.PDF)). 모두가 거부하는 정의가 회사가 가장 자주 공표하는 정의(SBC를 CFO 안에 더하고 리스 원금을 재무활동에 둔 CFO − capex)이므로, 정상화는 세 가지 기계적 차감(SBC, 금융리스 원금, 투자활동의 자본화 소프트웨어)과 한 가지 판단(유지 대 성장 capex)으로 요약된다 [INFERENCE].

### 수익률 분해: 조정 베이스 ROIC, ROIIC, DuPont

"Return on invested capital (ROIC) = Net operating profit after taxes (NOPAT) ÷ Invested capital (IC)"; NOPAT는 EBIT에서 시작해 "add amortization from acquired intangible assets (A) and the embedded interest component of operating lease expense ... Finally, you subtract cash taxes, which include the tax provision, deferred taxes, and the tax shield. NOPAT = EBITA – cash taxes". 투자자본은 영업접근(순운전자본 + 순PP&E + 취득무형자산 + 영업권 + 기타)과 재무접근이 있고 "we would select the operating approach if we had to pick one." 초과현금과 비영업자산은 제외한다. ROIIC = "Year1 NOPAT – Year0 NOPAT ÷ Year0 invested capital – Year-1 invested capital". 자사주 매입은 초과현금을 제거하면 ROIC에 영향이 없다: "buybacks have no impact on ROIC whatsoever... This is not true of ROE." 세그먼트: "First, the whole has to equal the sum of the parts." ([Mauboussin & Callahan ROIC 2022](https://www.eatonvance.com/content/dam/im/assets/publication/thought-leadership/consilient-observer/article_returnoninvestedcapital.pdf)). CFA 5단 DuPont: "Tax burden = Net income ÷ Earnings before taxes"; "Interest burden = Earnings before taxes ÷ Earnings before interest and taxes"; "EBIT margin"; "Total asset turnover ratio = Total revenue ÷ Average total assets"; "Financial leverage ratio (equity multiplier) = Average total assets ÷ Average shareholders' equity" ([CFA Institute 비율 목록](https://www.cfainstitute.org/sites/default/files/-/media/documents/support/programs/cfa/cfa%5Fprogram%5Flevel%5Fii%5Ffinancial%5Fratio%5Flist.pdf)). Novy-Marx는 매출총이익/자산을 쓰는 이유로 "gross profits are not reduced by interest payments and are thus independent of leverage"를 든다 ([NBER w15940](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w15940/w15940.pdf)).

### 유동성 원천과 용도: S&P의 숫자 그대로

S&P의 유동성 기준은 가장 구체적인 공개 규칙이다. "A/B: Liquidity sources (A) divided by uses (B). A-B: Liquidity sources (A) minus uses (B)." 원천: "Cash and liquid investments. Forecasted funds from operations (FFO), if positive. Forecasted working capital inflows, if positive. Proceeds of asset sales (when confidently predictable). The undrawn, available portion of committed credit facilities maturing beyond the next 12 months."; 커버넌트 제약: "we will only include the portion of committed credit facilities that we estimate is available without a covenant breach."; CP는 제외: "we do not include potential future CP issuance as a liquidity source since our liquidity analysis does not assume companies are able to borrow in debt capital markets". 용도: "Forecasted funds from operations, if negative. Expected capital spending. Forecasted working capital outflows, if negative. All debt maturities ... Credit puts that cause debt acceleration or new collateral posting requirements in the event of a downgrade of up to three notches. Contracted acquisitions and expected shareholder distributions under a stress scenario, including expected share repurchases." 등급: Exceptional "A/B of 2x or more projected each year over the next two years. Positive A-B, even if forecasted EBITDA were to decline by 50% ... debt is at least 30% below any covenant limits."; Strong "A/B for the upcoming 12 months of 1.5x or more and remaining above 1.0x over the subsequent 12-month period. Positive A-B, even if forecasted EBITDA declines by 30% ... debt is at least 25% below covenant limits."; Adequate "A/B of 1.2x or more over the upcoming 12 months ... Positive A-B, even if forecasted EBITDA declines by 15% ... debt is at least 15% below covenant limits" [PUBLISHED RULE] ([S&P 유동성 기준, Maalot 사본](https://www.maalot.co.il/Publications/MT20170309124234.pdf)). 레버리지 어휘(Table 17, 표준 변동성): FFO/debt 60+/45–60/30–45/20–30/12–20/<12%; Debt/EBITDA <1.5/1.5–2/2–3/3–4/4–5/>5x; FFO/현금이자 >13/9–13/6–9/4–6/2–4/<2; EBITDA/이자 >15/10–15/6–10/3–6/2–3/<2; CFO/debt >50/35–50/25–35/15–25/10–15/<10%; FOCF/debt 40+/25–40/15–25/10–15/5–10/<5; DCF/debt 25+/15–25/10–15/5–10/2–5/<2 [PUBLISHED RULE] ([S&P Corporate Methodology 2013, Maalot 사본](https://www.maalot.co.il/Publications/MT20190703113602.PDF)). 조정부채: "Incurred liabilities that provide no future offsetting operating benefit (such as underfunded postretirement employee benefits and asset retirement obligations); On- and off-balance-sheet commitments to purchase or use of long-life assets (such as lease obligations) ...; and Amounts relating to certain instances when a company accelerates the monetization of assets in lieu of borrowing" ([S&P 2019](https://www.maalot.co.il/Publications/MT20190402125127.PDF)). Moody's는 연금을 부채로("We reclassify as debt the pension liability recorded on the balance sheet.") 두되 OPEB는 제외한다 ([Moody's 69913](https://ratings.moodys.com/api/rmc-documents/69913)). 10-K에서 직접 읽을 수 있는 입력은 ASC 470-10-50-1의 5년 만기표, S-X 4-08의 디폴트·커버넌트 위반·면제 공시 ([PwC FSP 12.12](https://viewpoint.pwc.com/dt/us/en/pwc/accounting_guides/financial_statement_/financial_statement___18_US/chapter_12_debt_US/1212_disclosure_US.html)), ASC 205-40 계속기업 평가(경영진 계획은 "probable of implementation and probable of mitigating"일 때만 반영) ([BDO](https://arch.bdo.com/going-concern-assessments))다. 지연 신고(NT 10-K)의 정보가치: 사유 중 "Accounting Issues" 33%, 2일 CAR −2.66%, 설명이 없을 때 −4.2% ([Cao, Chen & Higgs, RAST 2016](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11142-016-9351-5)); 2026년 연구에서는 회계 사유 NT 이후 90거래일 내 4.02/10-K/A/10-Q/A 확률이 32.6%(잔여군 23.5%)다 ([Ahn, SSRN 2026](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6932478)). BIS 좀비 정의: 이자보상배율 1 미만 3년 연속, 업력 10년 이상 ([BIS 2018](https://www.bis.org/publ/qtrpdf/r_qt1809g.htm)).

### 커버넌트 EBITDA와 애드백 인플레이션

"The definition of EBITDA has always been a fundamental negotiation point in the leveraged finance market; ultimately, a legal construct as opposed to one derived from any recognised accounting standard." 상위 딜의 런레이트 기간은 "18-24 month periods become the standard", 캡은 "caps of 25% fast becoming the norm", 인증은 "the majority of such deals now not requiring any form of certification or verification; these only needing to be projected by borrowers 'in good faith'." ([White & Case, Synergising synergies](https://www.whitecase.com/sites/whitecase/files/files/download/publications/synergising-synergies.pdf)). EDGAR의 실제 조항: Arcosa 2029 인덴처는 "run rate" 비용절감을 "within 24 months"에 "reasonably identifiable and factually supportable (in the good faith determination of the Issuer)"이고 "shall not exceed 20.0% of the Consolidated EBITDA for such period (calculated before giving effect to any add-backs and adjustments pursuant to this clause (f))"로 제한한다 ([Arcosa 인덴처](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1739445/000173944521000039/exh41arcosa-indenture2029n.htm)); 다른 인덴처는 35% 캡에 Reg S-X 예외를 둔다 ([EX-4.3](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1610950/000119312520303717/d78619dex43.htm)). S&P의 6년 연구가 실증한다: "Addbacks represent a significant percentage of management-adjusted EBITDA at deal inception (30% on a median basis over the life of the study)"; "median leverage misses of 2.3 turns in year one following deal inception and 2.7 turns in year two"; "95% of the companies failed to meet their first-year projections and over 50% missed earnings projections by more than 33% in the two years following inception"; "The expansive definition of EBITDA has contributed to the rise in aggressive out-of-court restructurings in recent years." ([S&P Leveraged Finance Handbook 2024](https://sandpglobal-spglobal-live.cphostaccess.com/_assets/documents/ratings/research/101608150.pdf)). 2024년에 "Uncapped add-backs nearly disappeared, falling from 20% of add-back deals in Q3 2023 to 0% in Q3 2024." ([Thomson Reuters Legal](https://legal.thomsonreuters.com/blog/cost-savings-ebitda-addbacks/)). 재현 가능한 EX-10 읽기: "Consolidated EBITDA" 정의를 찾아 각 애드백을 GAAP 대조형·비현금형·일회성형·**예측형**으로 분류하고, 캡(%, 애드백 전/후 베이스, S-X 예외)·전망기간·증거기준·인증을 기록한 뒤, 헤드룸 = 1 − (최대 레버리지에서 요구되는 커버넌트 EBITDA ÷ 보고 커버넌트 EBITDA)를 예측형 애드백 제거 버전과 나란히 계산한다 [INFERENCE]. 애드백 비중이 연속 컴플라이언스 증명서에서 상승하면 그것 자체가 선행지표다 [INFERENCE].

### 자본배분 점검과 가이던스 대 첫 보고치

Mauboussin의 "golden rule": "A company should repurchase its shares only when its stock is trading below its expected value and when no better investment opportunities are available." "The shareholder rate of return on a buyback is the cost of equity divided by the ratio of stock price to intrinsic value." ([Credit Suisse 2014](https://www.shareholderforum.com/wag/Library/20140506_CreditSuisse.pdf)). SBC와 자사주의 결합: "if a company is both issuing via SBC and retiring via buybacks, the fundamental rule of capital allocation is at risk of being violated."; CFO의 68%가 희석 상쇄를 "important" 또는 "very important"로 답했다 ([CFA Society Toronto 2023](https://cfatoronto.ca/?p=5446)). 필링 기반 테스트: 평균 매입가 대 가치 추정, 순매입 = 총매입 − 발행(옵션 행사 포함), 순주식수 변화 대 SBC 비용 [INFERENCE]. 보수 공시는 Item 402(v)(2022년 8월 채택)로 표준화되어 "executive compensation actually paid"를 TSR·동종 TSR·순이익·회사선택 지표와 5년간 대조하고 "a list of three to seven financial performance measures"를 요구한다 ([SEC 2022-149](https://www.sec.gov/news/press-release/2022-149)). 내부자 거래는 2023년 Rule 10b5-1 체제(쿨링오프, 체크박스, 증여 Form 4 보고)에서 읽으며 ([SEC 2022-222](https://www.sec.gov/news/press-release/2022-222)), "A portfolio strategy that focuses solely on the remaining "opportunistic" traders yields value-weighted abnormal returns of 82 basis points per month, while abnormal returns associated with routine traders are essentially zero." ([Cohen, Malloy & Pomorski 2012](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/j.1540-6261.2012.01740.x)). 가이던스 대 실적의 기저율은 비트가 기본값이다: Q3 2025에 "83% have reported actual EPS above estimates, which is above the 5-year average of 78% and above the 10-year average of 75%... companies are reporting earnings that are 6.6% above estimates"; 부정 서프라이즈 반응 −5.0%(5년 평균 −2.6%) 대 긍정 +0.4% ([FactSet, 2025년 11월 21일](https://advantage.factset.com/hubfs/Website/Resources%20Section/Research%20Desk/Earnings%20Insight/EarningsInsight_112125.pdf)). 걷기 내리기(walk-down)는 "most pronounced when firms or insiders are net sellers of stock after an earnings announcement" ([Richardson, Teoh & Wysocki 2004](https://ideas.repec.org/a/wly/coacre/v21y2004i4p885-924.html)); "78% of the surveyed executives would give up economic value in exchange for smooth earnings" ([Graham, Harvey & Rajgopal, NBER w10550](https://www.nber.org/papers/w10550)). 따라서 비트/미스 이진 점수는 정보가 적고, 자기 과거 서프라이즈 분포 대비 크기와 분기 중 가이던스 수정 방향이 문헌이 지지하는 측정이다 [INFERENCE].

## 결론

다섯 묶음의 메모를 합치면 실무는 "스크린은 순위, 정독은 사건 파일, 공표는 모순뿐"이라는 세 층으로 정리된다. 스크린 층은 기계가 맡되 기말잔액·당기유량 규약, 팩토링·공급자금융 가산, 태그 안정성 점검이 없으면 신호가 아니라 잡음을 만든다. 정독 층의 산출물은 점수가 아니라 문서 위치에 묶인 이상 목록과 "what would disprove"이며, 어느 포렌식 기관도 기업 간 종합 점수를 공표하지 않는다. 공표 층에서 모순은 세 조건(빼기 후 잔차, 예정 반전의 실패, 독립 계열의 불일치) 중 하나가 충족될 때만 성립하고, SEC 코멘트레터의 문법("you attribute X to Y, yet Z lacks a corresponding category"; "quantify ... in absolute dollars"; "reconcile those terms to DSO")이 미해결 항목을 적는 가장 좋은 템플릿이다. 2023–2026년의 변화는 이 세 층의 입력을 바꿨다: 공급자금융·세그먼트 비용·관할권별 세금·가상자산·(2027년부터) 비용 분해가 태그로 들어오고, 집행 기저율은 급감해 통제 단독 신호의 사전확률이 낮아졌으며, 하이퍼스케일러 내용연수 연장이 Montier의 다섯 번째 플래그를 다시 중요하게 만들었다.

자동 분석가와 비교할 때 가장 먼저 확인할 지점은, 이 보고서에서 드러난 실무 규약이 파이프라인의 계산 규약과 일치하는가이다: DSO/DSI가 평균이 아닌 기말 잔액인지, 매각 채권과 공급자금융 의무가 일수와 레버리지에 가산되는지, 현금흐름 운전자본 라인의 재무상태표 대사 잔차가 "설명되지 않음"으로 남겨지는지, 롤포워드(충당금·보증·영업권·구조조정·계약부채·공급자금융)에서 적립÷사용과 전기추정변경이 계산되는지, 8-K 발표문과 10-Q의 라인별 diff와 전기 비교열의 little r diff가 있는지, 그리고 "모순" 라벨이 방향만으로 붙지 않고 크기·반전·독립 계열 중 하나를 요구하는지다. 이 보고서는 그 비교를 위한 기준선이며, 공백 목록은 별도로 이어진다.
